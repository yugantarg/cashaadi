Email support@cashaadi.in from the email address on your account. Tell us the date and amount of the payment and what went wrong. We usually reply within 2 working days.

The money goes back to your original payment method within 10 working days, and we confirm it by email.

No automatic renewals

Memberships are paid once for the year and do not renew automatically. You will not be charged again unless you choose to buy again.

If an account is closed for fraud or misuse of the platform, no refund is given.
